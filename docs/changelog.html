Changelog

What's new.

Lintel updates itself; here's what each version brought.

Lintel 0.1.6 Latest

13 November 2026

Polish

  • The welcome guide covers Claude Code status and Today, and lets you turn on the weather, the System Monitor and clipboard history from the start.
  • VoiceOver reads Today and the clipboard history more clearly, and announces when you copy an item again.
  • Tested for 40 minutes with every feature running at once: no leaks, steady memory.

Lintel 0.1.5

13 November 2026

Stays out of the way

  • The island steps aside while a video, presentation or game is full screen, and comes back when you leave. Notices still appear. Turn it off in Settings › Island.

Clipboard history

  • Press ⌃⌥V to see the last 12 things you copied, and click one to copy it again. Kept in memory only, never saved; passwords are never recorded. Turn it on in Settings › Activities › Clipboard.

Make it yours

  • An outline around the island, in a colour you choose.
  • A soft glow in the album's colour while music plays.
  • Size presets, and a choice of what shows beside the camera when several things are live.

Lintel 0.1.4

13 November 2026

Today

  • Open the island with nothing playing to see the date, your next event and, if you turn it on, the weather where you are. Turn on the weather in Settings › Activities › Today.

Screenshots to the shelf

  • New screenshots and screen recordings can land on the shelf by themselves, ready to drag into any app or AirDrop. Turn it on in Settings › Activities › Shelf and choose your screenshots folder (usually the Desktop).

Lintel 0.1.3

13 November 2026

System Monitor

  • CPU, memory, network, battery health and disk in the island, with graphs of the last few minutes, your local IP address and a speed test. Turn it on in Settings › Activities.
  • Quiet by default: the notch stays clear until the CPU stays busy, memory runs short or a big download runs. Open the island, or press the gauge button from any activity, to see the panel.
  • Alerts when the CPU stays busy for two minutes, macOS runs short of memory, or the disk is nearly full.

Fixes

  • The mirror no longer stays black the first time you open it.

Lintel 0.1.2

6 November 2026

Claude Code, clearer

  • The expanded island now shows Claude's last few steps on their own full-width lines, so nothing is cut off.
  • Each Claude session in the list names its project.
  • A Claude timer no longer keeps running after you interrupt Claude, or when a turn ends on an error. Lintel now tells you why it stopped, for example "rate limit reached".

Lighter

  • The working spinner uses about a tenth of the CPU it did.

Lintel 0.1.1

6 November 2026

Claude Code in the notch, and lighter on memory.

  • Claude Code status: while Claude Code works (Terminal, VS Code or the Claude app's Code tab), the island shows the project, the current step and the time so far, tells you when Claude needs your input, and says when it's done. It sets itself up automatically when Claude Code is installed; Lintel says so once, and you can turn it off with one click or in Settings.
  • Script notifications (lintel://notify) are about 10 times faster.
  • Fixed memory growth from frequent events, and at most 5 notifications now wait their turn, so a flood can't pile up.
  • The project folder is labelled clearly in Claude's status, and other small fixes.

Not notarized yet: if macOS asks, open Lintel from System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway.

Lintel 0.1.0

6 November 2026

First public release of Lintel: live activities around your Mac's notch.

  • Live activities beside the notch: timers and stopwatches, Pomodoro focus, Now Playing for Music and Spotify, charging and battery, downloads, volume, calendar meetings, countdowns to a date, reminders.
  • A drag-and-drop shelf on the notch, with AirDrop, Quick Look, compress, convert, resize, combine into PDF, pinning, search and multi-select.
  • A quick note anywhere with Control-Option-N.
  • Network, VPN, Low Power Mode, unlock and Bluetooth notices.
  • Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight actions, and a lintel:// URL scheme for scripts.
  • Updates itself.

Not notarized yet: the first time, open Lintel, click Done, then System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway (on macOS 14, right-click Lintel.app › Open). Updates after that install without asking.
